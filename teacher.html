    How to use in class

    1. Set it up. Pick the level, year level and options, then press Copy link.
    2. Share it. Paste the link into your LMS or email, or show this QR code on the projector. Students on phones or Chromebooks scan it and land on the same view.
    3. Present. Use presentation mode on the projector, and press P to hide the side panel and give the cell the whole screen.
    4. Pause to ask. Tick "Start paused" or press Pause on the stage to freeze the cell while you ask a question, then press Play.
    5. Keep it fair. Lock the year level so every student sees the same wording, or hide quizzes for a first look.
